# wavNotes

A personal listening notebook for studying music through close listening and free-form observations, without requiring music-theory knowledge.

## Language

**Project**:
One audio recording together with the listener's annotations about that recording.
_Avoid_: Session, workspace

**Recording**:
The audio being studied in a project.
_Avoid_: Track, song

**Annotation**:
A free-form formatted note attached to a moment or passage in the recording. Separate annotations may refer to the same time.

**Point annotation**:
An annotation attached to a single timestamp in the recording.

**Range annotation**:
An annotation attached to a passage with a start and end timestamp.

**Marker**:
The visible indicator of a point annotation on the timeline, not a separate container for notes.

**Project note**:
A free-form formatted note for observations about the recording as a whole, without a timestamp or time range.

**Selection**:
A temporary passage chosen for listening or creating a range annotation. A selection is not itself a saved annotation.

**Playhead**:
The current playback position in the recording.

**Focus queue**:
Annotations captured for later writing, worked through one at a time after the listener pauses playback.
_Avoid_: Playlist

**Pending annotation**:
An annotation awaiting completion in the focus queue, whether or not it already contains some writing.

**Resume position**:
The position where the listener paused to begin working through the focus queue and can return to afterward.
